Our story

Inspired by Dubai, shared across the Emirates.

Küche is a brand of Tili Star Trading, a UAE food company that has delivered to stores in all seven emirates since 2019.

We started with the flavour everyone was talking about, pistachio and kunafa wrapped in rich chocolate, and built a whole family of treats around it.
